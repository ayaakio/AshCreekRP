Business Charter

Businesses are one of the foundations of Ash Creek RP. They provide employment, create stories and bring the territory to life.

These guidelines exist to ensure every business contributes positively to the community and remains fair for all players.

Chapter I

Business Applications

1.1

Business Applications

All player-owned businesses must be applied for through the official Ash Creek RP Discord. Businesses created without staff approval are not recognised as official server businesses.

1.2

Business Proposal Requirements

Every application must include a complete business proposal for staff review.

At a minimum, the proposal must include the business name, business type, services offered, intended location, Standard Operating Procedures, rank structure.

1.3

Complete Applications

Applications that are missing required information may be declined or returned to the applicant for revision.

Applicants should provide enough detail for staff to understand how the business will operate and what roleplay it will create.

1.4

Original Business Concepts

Business concepts should be original, believable and appropriate for the Ash Creek setting. Businesses should offer meaningful roleplay and contribute something useful to the community.

1.5

Staff Review

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval. Staff may consider server needs, existing businesses, available locations, roleplay opportunities and the overall quality of the proposal.

1.6

Business Locations and MLOs

Custom MLOs are not available for player-owned businesses. All proposals must use an existing location already available within Ash Creek RP.

Applicants should select a suitable location as part of their proposal. Staff may approve the chosen location or recommend an alternative where appropriate.

Chapter II

Business Ownership

2.1

Active Ownership

Business owners are expected to actively manage and participate in their business. Owning a business is an ongoing roleplay responsibility, not simply a title or source of income.

2.2

Fourteen-Day Inactivity Rule

A business owner who remains inactive for fourteen consecutive days without providing prior notice may have their business removed.

The business may then be reopened for new applications or placed under new management.

2.3

Planned Absence

Owners who know they will be away should notify staff before their absence. Reasonable accommodations may be made when prior notice has been provided.

2.4

Leadership Responsibilities

Business owners are responsible for managing their employees, maintaining their approved procedures and ensuring that staff members understand both server rules and business expectations.

2.5

Rank Structure

Businesses must follow the rank structure approved in their proposal. Any major changes to management roles or leadership structure should be discussed with staff.

2.6

Ownership Changes

Business ownership may not be transferred, sold or gifted to another player without prior staff approval.

Chapter III

Business Operations

3.1

Roleplay First

Businesses exist to generate roleplay for the community. Owners and employees should focus on creating interactions, employment and stories rather than only maximising profit.

3.2

Follow Approved Procedures

Businesses should operate according to the Standard Operating Procedures included in their approved proposal.

Significant changes to the way a business operates should be discussed with staff before being introduced.

3.3

Fair and Reasonable Pricing

Prices should remain reasonable and suitable for the server economy. Businesses must not use unrealistic or excessive pricing in a way that harms the wider roleplay experience.

3.4

Employee Conduct

Employees should represent their business appropriately while on duty. This includes treating customers respectfully, following business procedures and maintaining believable roleplay.

3.5

Regular Business Activity

Approved businesses should open and operate regularly. Businesses that remain consistently inactive may be reviewed to determine whether they continue to benefit the community.

3.6

Business Locations

Businesses must operate from their approved location unless staff have permitted an alternative arrangement.

Players must not claim ownership of another property or location without approval.

Chapter IV

Staff Expectations

4.1

Staff Decisions

Business-related staff decisions are made to maintain a fair, balanced and enjoyable server economy.

Any disagreement should be raised respectfully through the appropriate Ash Creek RP Discord channels.

4.2

Business Reviews

Staff may review a business when concerns arise regarding inactivity, management, rule violations or its overall contribution to the community.

4.3

Rule Violations

Repeated or serious violations may result in warnings, changes to management, temporary suspension or removal of the business.

4.4

Businesses Are a Privilege

Operating an official business is a privilege, not an automatic right. Owners are expected to maintain the standards outlined in their approved proposal and in these rules.

4.5

The Community Comes First

Business decisions should support the wider Ash Creek RP community. Owners should consider how their actions affect employees, customers, other businesses and the server economy.

Official Town Council Notice

Approval Carries Responsibility

By accepting ownership or management of an official Ash Creek RP business, you agree to remain active, follow your approved proposal and operate in a way that benefits the wider community.
